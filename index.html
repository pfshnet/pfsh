What is PFSH?

PFSH (pronounced fish) is a decentralized community of hackers, dreamers, and disruptors. Inspired by the fluid chaos of Niven's Permanent Floating Riot Club, we believe in the power of unbridled creativity, unregulated experimentation, and swimming against the tide. Together, we hack the world, not to break it, but to reimagine it.

Hacker Philosophy

  • Anarchy with Purpose: Disruption isn’t destruction, it’s evolution.
  • Fluidity: Like fish, we move through networks, adapt, and grow.
  • Decentralization: No hierarchies, no silos, just a web of shared knowledge.
  • Transparency and Openness: We build in the open, for everyone.
  • Creativity Through Chaos: Out of the riot comes innovation.
